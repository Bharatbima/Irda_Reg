# Compliance Cost/Benefit Ledger

## COST #1 (confirmed real): Mis-selling / suitability tagging system (Section 11)
Genuinely new requirement — PIR functional-ID seller-tagging, suitability documentation, mis-selling claw-back exposure. Requires both a tech/system build AND compliant people/process. Applies most acutely to high-volume, low-touch API-style models (NBFC Layer 1, tractor/OEM) where there's no human advisor naturally generating this documentation as a byproduct of the sales conversation.

## BENEFIT #1 (confirmed real): Faster commission payment (Section 9, para 130)
Mandate: commission payable day-after-free-look, ~7-day insurer target, interest penalty for delay. Confirmed current baseline: T+30-45 days actual payment lag today. This is a material working-capital improvement, especially for high-volume thin-margin models (NBFC/tractor embedded plays) where cash conversion cycle matters more than for advisory/commercial lines.

## NON-ISSUES (confirmed closed, do not revisit)
- **Direct premium flow (Section 9, para 126)**: Non-issue — Bharat Bima does not collect/pool premium in any current or planned model.
- **Dark-pattern / pre-collection-of-customer-info rules**: Not currently applicable — no consumer digital lead-gen operation today. Worth noting for any future model reference only.
- **Capital requirements (Section 6)**: Non-issue — already capitalised at ₹1cr.
- **Training escalation (Section 6)**: Non-issue — training is paper-only formality in practice, both before and after the reform.
- **Open architecture (Section 6)**: Non-issue — brokers are already open-architecture by default; no real choice existed to begin with.
- **PoSP exclusivity (Section 6)**: Non-issue — exclusivity is easily circumvented in practice via re-registration under another name.
- **MII / Bima Sugam option (Sections 12-14)**: Explicitly not being pursued — direct business-direction instruction: "we are not going anywhere near the MII option its not for us." This is not a compliance-display/coexistence question to solve for; it is simply not a channel Bharat Bima is building toward. Any dealer-side MII/QR display obligations (e.g. para 142) are the OEM/dealer's compliance burden, not a strategic question for Bharat Bima.

## Net read so far
One clear new cost (mis-selling tagging build), one clear new benefit (cash flow), and a lengthening list of things that looked like impacts on paper but wash out in practice. The real economic story is not in these compliance mechanics — it's in the commission/EoM compression covered in the section-by-section log and the model theses (affinity, tractor/OEM, commercial lines).
