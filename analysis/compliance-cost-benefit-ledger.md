# Compliance Cost/Benefit Ledger

## COST — Mis-selling / suitability tagging system (Section 11)
A genuinely new requirement: PIR functional-ID seller-tagging, suitability documentation, and mis-selling claw-back exposure. Requires both a technology build and compliant people/process. Applies most acutely to high-volume, low-touch API-style models (NBFC Layer 1, tractor/OEM) where there's no human advisor naturally generating this documentation as a byproduct of the sales conversation.

## BENEFIT — Faster commission payment (Section 9, para 130)
Commission is payable no later than the day after the free-look period ends, with insurers targeting ~7-day payment and interest penalties for delay. Confirmed current baseline: T+30-45 days actual payment lag today. This is a material working-capital improvement, especially for high-volume, thin-margin models (NBFC/tractor embedded plays) where cash conversion cycle matters more than for advisory/commercial lines.

## NON-ISSUES (no material impact on Bharat Bima)
- **Direct premium flow (Section 9, para 126):** Bharat Bima does not collect or pool premium in any current or planned model.
- **Dark-pattern / pre-collection-of-customer-info rules:** not currently applicable — no consumer digital lead-gen operation today. Worth noting for any future model.
- **Capital requirements (Section 6):** already capitalised at ₹1cr, above any relevant threshold.
- **Training escalation (Section 6):** training compliance is a documentation exercise in practice both before and after the reform.
- **Open architecture (Section 6):** brokers are already open-architecture by default — no real choice existed to begin with.
- **PoSP exclusivity (Section 6):** exclusivity is easily circumvented in practice via re-registration under another name.
- **MII / Bima Sugam (Sections 12-14):** not a channel Bharat Bima is pursuing. Any dealer-side MII/QR display obligations (e.g. para 142) fall on the OEM/dealer, not on Bharat Bima.

## Net read
One clear new cost (mis-selling tagging build), one clear new benefit (cash flow), and a lengthening list of items that read as impacts on paper but wash out in practice. The real economic story isn't in these compliance mechanics — it's in the commission/EoM compression covered in the section-by-section findings and the business model theses (affinity, tractor/OEM, commercial lines).
